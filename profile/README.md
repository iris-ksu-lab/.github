# 🔬 IRIS KSU Lab

**AI-powered, smartphone-based retinal imaging for early, affordable diabetic eye disease detection**

Kennesaw State University · College of Computing and Software Engineering

---

## About Us

IRIS KSU Lab is a research group at Kennesaw State University, led by
**Dr. Mahmut Karakaya** and **Dr. Ramazan Aygun**, developing an
**NIH-funded, smartphone-based retinal imaging system** to detect
**diabetic retinopathy** earlier and more affordably using artificial
intelligence.

Diabetic retinopathy is a complication of diabetes that damages the retina's blood vessels and can cause vision loss without early symptoms. It affects more than 9 million people in the U.S. alone. Traditional screening equipment
can cost as much as $50,000. Our approach instead pairs a smartphone, a lens attachment, and AI to capture a retinal image, assess its quality, and analyze it for signs of disease flagging cases that need a specialist follow-up.

## Research Areas

- 🩺 **DR Detection** — CNN and attention-based models for diabetic
  retinopathy classification from fundus and smartphone-based retinal images
- 📷 **Image Quality Assessment** — gradability and reliability of
  point-of-care smartphone retinal imaging
